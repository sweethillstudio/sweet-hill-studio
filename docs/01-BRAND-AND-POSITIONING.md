# SWEET HILL STUDIO
## Brand & Positioning Master Document
### Version 0.1

---

## 1. PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT

This document defines the strategic identity, positioning, audience, personality and language of Sweet Hill Studio.

It is intended to guide:

- website design
- website copy
- product presentation
- publishing decisions
- editorial tone
- AI coding agents
- AI copywriting agents
- designers
- future marketing activity
- future authors and collaborators

This document is subordinate only to formally approved business decisions and should be treated as the default source of truth for brand behaviour.

---

# 2. BRAND NAME

**Sweet Hill Studio**

Primary domain:

**www.sweethillstudio.com**

The brand name should always be presented consistently as:

**Sweet Hill Studio**

Do not unnecessarily append:

- Publishing
- Books
- Kids
- Press
- Foundation
- International
- India

The brand should be capable of becoming substantially larger than its first books.

# 3. WHAT SWEET HILL STUDIO IS

Sweet Hill Studio is an independent publishing and creative studio developing beautifully made books and related storytelling experiences for children and families.

Its initial publishing direction centres on stories arising from the Vedic, Vaiṣṇava and broader Indic knowledge and storytelling traditions.

The long-term ambition is larger than a catalogue of children's books.

Sweet Hill Studio is intended to develop an interconnected publishing and intellectual-property ecosystem involving:

- books
- authors
- series
- characters
- stories
- educational resources
- multiple languages
- audio
- animation
- licensing
- future media
- cultural storytelling

The website must therefore communicate that Sweet Hill Studio is a **publishing house in development**, not simply an online store.

---

# 4. THE CENTRAL BRAND IDEA

## Contemporary publishing. Timeless stories.

Sweet Hill Studio should sit at the intersection of:

**STORY**

**CULTURE**

**CHILDHOOD**

**CRAFT**

**EDUCATION**

**TECHNOLOGY**

The brand should make traditional stories feel:

- alive
- beautiful
- relevant
- accessible
- emotionally engaging
- worthy of careful attention

without making them feel artificially modernised or culturally diluted.

---

# 5. WHAT SWEET HILL STUDIO IS NOT

The brand must not be positioned as:

### A generic children's brand

Do not rely on childish visual language, excessive cartoon styling or nursery aesthetics.

### A generic religious bookstore

The website should not resemble a temple shop or catalogue of devotional products.

### An academic Sanskrit publisher

Cultural and scriptural depth matters, but the primary experience is storytelling and publishing rather than academic study.

### An AI-content company

AI may be used as a production tool, but AI is not the brand proposition.

### A low-cost mass-content operation

The emphasis is on thoughtful curation, storytelling quality, visual quality and cultural integrity.

### A conventional start-up brand

Avoid excessive corporate language such as:

- disruption
- scale
- solutions
- innovation ecosystem
- next-generation platform

unless genuinely relevant.

# 7. BRAND PROMISE

Sweet Hill Studio should promise:

### Beauty

Books that are visually compelling and made with care.

### Story

Stories that are engaging rather than merely instructional.

### Cultural integrity

Respect for the source traditions, names, concepts and narratives.

### Accessibility

Stories presented in language and formats families can actually enjoy.

### Trust

Parents, educators and communities should understand where the stories come from and how they have been developed.

### Discovery

The publishing experience should lead naturally from one book or story into another.

# 10. CORE AUDIENCES

## 10.1 Parents and grandparents

Primary purchase decision-makers.

They need:

- beauty
- trust
- suitability
- age guidance
- cultural confidence
- quality
- clear purchasing information

They should quickly understand:

**What is this?**

**Is it suitable for my child?**

**Why is this book worth having?**

---

## 10.2 Children

Children are an important emotional audience even though the website's commerce interface is primarily designed for adults.

Children should encounter:

- wonder
- characters
- visual richness
- stories
- exploration
- discovery

The website must not infantilise adults simply because children are involved.

---

## 10.3 Educators

Includes:

- schools
- gurukulas
- homeschoolers
- temple education
- teachers
- parents using books educationally

They need:

- age guidance
- educational context
- discussion material
- activities
- teaching resources
- group ordering
- source information

---

## 10.4 Vaiṣṇava / ISKCON / Vedic communities

This audience is likely to be important initially.

They need:

- confidence in cultural and scriptural authenticity
- appropriate terminology
- respect for sampradāya context
- transparency about sources
- clarity about adaptation and presentation

The website should serve this audience without making the whole brand look like a sectarian storefront.

---

## 10.5 General global families

Many customers will have little or no prior familiarity with Vedic traditions.

The site must therefore explain concepts without:

- assuming prior knowledge
- overloading visitors with Sanskrit terminology
- flattening the tradition into generic "mythology"

Cultural depth and accessibility must coexist.

---

## 10.6 Booksellers, distributors and institutions

These users need:

- professional publisher information
- bibliographic data
- formats
- ISBNs
- ordering information
- contact pathways
- wholesale information

This must exist without overwhelming ordinary family shoppers.

---

# 11. CULTURAL AND SCRIPTURAL POSITIONING

Sweet Hill Studio should treat its source traditions with intellectual and cultural seriousness.

Where relevant, the site should identify:

- source texts
- traditional authors
- Sanskrit names
- transliteration
- sampradāya context
- adaptation choices

Do not describe authoritative Vedic texts as "myths", "mythological stories" or "mythical texts" as the brand's own terminology.

Use contextually appropriate language such as:

- Vedic literature
- śāstra
- Itihāsa
- Purāṇa
- sacred text
- traditional narrative
- devotional literature
- Vaiṣṇava literature
- source text

When a modern audience requires explanation, provide the explanation without compromising the traditional framing.

---

# 12. LANGUAGE AND NAMING

Use accurate Sanskrit transliteration where appropriate.

Preserve diacritics for formal titles and names when established.

Examples:

- Kṛṣṇa
- Nārada
- Dvārakā
- Uṣā
- Aniruddha
- Śrī
- Bhāgavatāmṛta
- Gauḍīya
- Vaiṣṇava

For accessibility, longer or highly technical terminology may be accompanied by plain-English explanation.

Do not remove diacritics merely because an AI system finds them inconvenient.

Search-engine accessibility and cultural accuracy must be solved technically rather than by degrading the official terminology.

# 13. FIRST PUBLISHED TITLES

The current Sweet Hill Studio catalogue includes at least two titles available through print-on-demand channels.

## 13.1 Śrī Bāla Bhāgavatāmṛta – Part 1: Nārada’s Quest for the Greatest Devotee

Author:

**Kirti-kumārī dāsī**

The book is a children's adaptation inspired by Śrī Sanātana Gosvāmī's Bṛhad-Bhāgavatāmṛta.

Its narrative follows Devarṣi Nārada in his search to understand who is the greatest devotee.

The book introduces children to a sequence of devotees and personalities associated with the journey, including figures such as:

- the Pāṇḍavas
- Prahlāda
- Hanumān
- Lord Śiva
- Lord Brahmā

The website presentation must distinguish carefully between:

- the original source text
- the children's adaptation
- the author's storytelling and editorial decisions
- the visual interpretation

Do not present the children's adaptation as a literal reproduction of the source text.

Sweet Hill Studio is intended to operate through a connected NZ + India model.

The customer-facing brand is one:

**Sweet Hill Studio**

The two operational centres may eventually have distinct responsibilities.

### New Zealand

Potential responsibilities include:

- global creative direction
- publishing leadership
- authors
- editorial
- international relationships
- website
- global brand
- international markets

### India

Potential responsibilities include:

- printing
- production
- inventory
- warehousing
- Indian distribution

- educational resources

# 20. WEBSITE POSITIONING

The website should feel like:

> **A contemporary global publishing house that happens to create extraordinary books for children.**

It should not feel like:

> a children's ecommerce template

or:

> a religious bookstore with a shopping cart.

The site should combine:

**Editorial + Commerce + Culture + Technology**

without allowing technology to dominate the experience.

---

# 21. CONTENT TONE

Website copy should be:

- clear
- elegant
- intelligent
- accessible
- concise where possible
- evocative where appropriate
- culturally respectful
- confident without being boastful

Prefer:

> Discover the world of Nārada as he travels from devotee to devotee, searching for the heart of bhakti.

Avoid:

> Embark on an AMAZING FUN-FILLED SPIRITUAL ADVENTURE!!!

---

# 22. SELL WITHOUT SOUNDING LIKE ADVERTISING

The website should communicate value without excessive sales language.

Prefer:

- Discover
- Explore
- Read the Story
- Meet the Characters
- Inside the Book
- For Educators
- Coming Soon
- Explore the Series

Use stronger commerce language where appropriate:

- Add to Cart
- Buy the Book
- Choose Edition
- View Details

Avoid:

- LIMITED TIME!!!
- MUST BUY!!!
- AMAZING!!!
- BEST EVER!!!
- DON'T MISS OUT!!!

unless a genuine campaign specifically requires such language.

---

# 23. AI TRANSPARENCY POSITION

Sweet Hill Studio may use AI-assisted tools in parts of the creative production process.

The brand position should be transparent while making clear that:

- authorship remains human
- creative direction remains human
- editorial decisions remain human
- cultural/scriptural responsibility remains human
- AI tools may assist with visual production and other workflow stages

Do not frame AI use as the central marketing proposition.

Do not hide material AI involvement.

Create a dedicated explanation page/section where appropriate.

---

# 24. TRUST ARCHITECTURE

Trust should be established through:

- identifiable authors
- publisher information
- clear source information
- accurate book details
- transparent AI disclosure where relevant
- clear contact information
- secure checkout
- professional policies
- shipping information
- returns information
- privacy information
- accessible customer support

Trust should never depend on exaggerated claims.

---

# 25. BRAND VISUAL PRINCIPLES

The interface should use:

- sophisticated typography
- generous whitespace
- restrained UI
- strong editorial composition
- high-quality illustration
- controlled colour
- intelligent grid systems
- subtle motion
- clear hierarchy

The interface should avoid:

- childish decoration
- generic cartoon UI
- visual noise
- excessive cards
- excessive rounded corners
- excessive gradients
- low-quality stock imagery
- generic AI artwork used as UI decoration

The illustrations and books provide the wonder.

The interface provides the structure.

---

# 26. BRAND EXPERIENCE TEST

The brand should pass the following tests:

### Test A
Does it look like a credible publishing house?

### Test B
Does it feel contemporary rather than dated?

### Test C
Does it feel culturally authentic without looking sectarian?

### Test D
Can an unfamiliar international parent understand what the company does?

### Test E
Can a knowledgeable devotee see that the source traditions have been treated seriously?

### Test F
Does the website remain sophisticated even when showing children's content?

---

# 28. BRAND NORTH STAR

When a design, copy, feature or business decision is uncertain, ask:

> Does this help Sweet Hill Studio become a respected, beautiful and enduring publishing house?

If the answer is no, question the decision.

---

# 29. CONTENT AUTHORSHIP RULE

AI systems may assist with:

- research organisation
- drafting
- editing
- metadata
- technical implementation
- content formatting

but must not invent:

- author biographies
- source-text details
- scriptural claims
- publication facts
- ISBN information
- quotes
- reviews
- testimonials
- customer claims
- institutional affiliations

Any missing authoritative information must be marked as requiring verification.

---

# 30. FINAL BRAND PRINCIPLE

Sweet Hill Studio should feel like a place where:

**beautiful books are made,**

**stories are discovered,**

**children encounter wonder,**

**parents find something they trust,**

**educators find something useful,**

and

**timeless cultural traditions are presented with care.**

The brand should remain contemporary without becoming disposable, and traditional without becoming visually dated.
