About Us

The Studio

Sweet Hill Studio is an independent children’s publishing studio with creative roots in New Zealand and a growing production base in India. We exist to ensure that stories from the Vedic and Vaiṣṇava literary traditions are presented with the visual quality, accessibility, and professionalism expected by contemporary parents and children.

How We Create

Every Sweet Hill Studio book is written, directed, edited, curated, and culturally contextualised by human creators. To achieve our highly cinematic illustration style, we utilise generative AI as an iterative production tool. This process is strictly guided by human art direction to ensure character consistency, cultural authenticity, devotional mood, and scriptural accuracy. AI is our brush, not our author.

From Śāstra to Story

We adapt living Vaiṣṇava textual traditions into accessible children’s storytelling. Our adaptations are crafted with deep respect for the source texts, sampradāya distinctions, and devotional vocabulary. We clearly distinguish between the original śāstra and our educational adaptations, ensuring parents and educators understand the exact scriptural provenance of every book we publish.
